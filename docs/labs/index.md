# Labs

Labs turn theory into reproducible evidence analysis.

## Lab standard

Each public lab should record:

1. the question or objective;
2. the artifact examined;
3. the exact location or structure;
4. the command or tool used;
5. the relevant raw value or bytes;
6. the interpretation;
7. what the evidence establishes;
8. what it does not establish;
9. uncertainties or corroboration opportunities.

The goal is reproducibility rather than an answer-key dump.
