---
title: Forensic Methodology
description: Preserve evidence, identify structure, inspect bytes, validate interpretations, and document conclusions.
---

Digital-forensic conclusions should be traceable to evidence and to the structure used to interpret that evidence.

~~~text
preserve
  ↓
identify artifact and address space
  ↓
apply documented structure
  ↓
inspect relevant bytes or values
  ↓
interpret
  ↓
corroborate independently
  ↓
record conclusion and limits
~~~

Carrier separates preservation, evidence searching, and event reconstruction, and recommends preservation, isolation, correlation, and logging when developing procedures.

## Evidence before interpretation

For an important finding, be able to state:

- what artifact was examined;
- relative to what base the location was measured;
- which structure defines the field;
- what bytes or values were actually observed;
- what transformation or parser produced the human-readable result;
- what second observation supports the interpretation.
