---
title: Hashing and Verification
description: Compare evidence byte streams without confusing a digest match with a case conclusion.
---

A cryptographic hash maps an input byte stream to a fixed-size digest. A forensic verification workflow compares a digest calculated from the evidence being examined with a digest recorded for the verification object.

~~~text
evidence bytes
  ↓
hash function
  ↓
calculated digest
  ↓
compare with recorded digest
  ↓
match or mismatch
~~~

The CFReDS training lab calculates MD5 and SHA-1 because those are the verification values used by the exercise. A mismatch is a stop condition until the discrepancy is explained.

A hash match does not identify partitions, users, activity, or filesystem contents.
