---
title: Validation and Corroboration
description: Cross-check important interpretations against independent representations or sources.
---

Carrier's correlation principle is useful at both the investigation level and the structure-decoding level.

~~~text
tool interpretation
        ↓
raw-byte inspection
        ↓
documented field layout
        ↓
manual or deterministic decoding
        ↓
comparison
~~~

Examples include comparing an MBR starting-LBA field with `fdisk`, computing a partition offset from the observed sector size, and using `fsstat` instead of treating partition type `0x07` as proof of NTFS.

When sources disagree, preserve the disagreement and investigate it rather than choosing the more convenient value.
