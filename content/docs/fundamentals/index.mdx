These pages contain concepts reused across investigations. Lab pages apply them to evidence rather than redefining them.

~~~text
artifact
  ↓
documented structure
  ↓
raw evidence
  ↓
interpretation
  ↓
independent validation
  ↓
conclusion
~~~

A forensic parser is interpretation machinery. Its output can be useful, but the parser is not the underlying evidence.
