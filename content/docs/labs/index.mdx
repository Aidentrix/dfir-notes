Labs turn theory into reproducible evidence analysis.

## Lab standard

Each public lab should record the question or objective, artifact examined, exact location or structure, command or tool used, relevant raw value or bytes, interpretation, what the evidence establishes, what it does not establish, and remaining uncertainties.

The goal is reproducibility rather than an answer-key dump.
