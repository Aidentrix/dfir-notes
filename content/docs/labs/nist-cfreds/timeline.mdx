## Shutdown time

SYSTEM\ControlSet001\Control\Windows\ShutdownTime contained:

~~~text
57 a9 48 b5 10 67 d0 01
~~~

Decoded as little-endian Windows FILETIME:

~~~text
2015-03-25 15:31:05.232008 UTC
~~~

## Interpretation boundary

The shutdown record occurred after the DHCP lease was obtained and before the lease's T1 and expiration times.

That temporal relationship is supported. It does **not** by itself establish a causal relationship between the shutdown and the network state.
